## Done recently

**0.8.6**

- **Item shapes read from your own install**: The true shapes of game items the stores need are measured from your own Valheim install on first start, instead of shipping in the download. Same look; nothing from the game's files in the mod.
- **Unofficial-mod notice in game**: The mod says in game that it is unofficial and not affiliated with Iron Gate or Coffee Stain, as Iron Gate asks every mod to.
- **Brewer's Cabinet takes BarleyWineBase** ([#2](https://github.com/joostliebregts/valheim-storehouse/issues/2)): The cabinet takes the barley wine base as well as the finished barley wine (the base was refused).
- **Meads and bases from other mods** ([#2](https://github.com/joostliebregts/valheim-storehouse/issues/2)): The Brewer's Cabinet recognises meads and mead bases from any mod, by asking the game's Fermenter what goes in and what comes out.
- **Stores open where the items are** ([#2](https://github.com/joostliebregts/valheim-storehouse/issues/2)): A store opens scrolled to where its items are: at the bottom when the game put them there, at the top when a sorting mod moved them up, at the top when it's empty.
- **Main menu says Storehouse loaded** ([#1](https://github.com/joostliebregts/valheim-storehouse/issues/1)): A line at the top left of the main menu says Storehouse loaded with all its pieces, so players can check before they load a world after an update. A failed load shows a red warning; no line at all means it did not load.
